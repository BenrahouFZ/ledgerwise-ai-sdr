# Fictional client: BrightDesk

BrightDesk sells customer support software to small and medium businesses.

Plans: Starter costs 29 USD per user per month. Pro costs 59 USD per user per month. Enterprise has custom pricing.

The Pro plan includes live chat, email ticketing, reporting dashboards, and integrations with Slack and HubSpot.

Sales process: a lead fills a form, a sales rep qualifies the lead in 24 hours, then a demo call is booked, then a proposal is sent.

Ideal customer: companies with 10 to 200 employees that receive more than 500 support requests per month.
