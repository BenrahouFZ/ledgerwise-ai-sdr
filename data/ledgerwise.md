Ledgerwise is a US bookkeeping firm.

Ledgerwise does monthly bookkeeping for e-commerce brands.

Typical Ledgerwise clients sell online through Shopify, Amazon or their own website, with roughly 1 to 10 million dollars in yearly revenue and a team of 5 to 50 people.

Ledgerwise does not discuss pricing by email. Pricing is discussed on a call with the sales team.

The next step for interested brands is a short intro call with a member of the Ledgerwise sales team.
